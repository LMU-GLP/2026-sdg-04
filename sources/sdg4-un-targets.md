# SDG 4: Quality Education — Official UN Targets and Indicators

- **Source:** United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, Goal 4 (Quality Education)
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures and targets

- **Target 4.1** (primary/secondary completion): "In 2024, completion rates reached 88% in primary, 78% in lower secondary, and 61% in upper secondary education."
- **Target 4.2** (early childhood education): "Approximately two-thirds of young children are developmentally on track globally."
- **Target 4.3** (technical/vocational/tertiary access): "One sixth of individuals between the ages of 15 and 64 recently participated in formal and non-formal education and training."
- **Target 4.5** (eliminate gender/other disparities — the target most relevant to "students who face barriers"): indicator 4.5.1 tracks parity indices "for all education indicators," including by **disability status**. "The wealth parity index for completion rates is 0.91 in primary, 0.68 in lower secondary, and 0.34 in upper secondary education" — i.e., the gap between richest and poorest students widens sharply at higher levels.
- **Target 4.6** (literacy/numeracy): "Some 754 million adults worldwide remained illiterate in 2024, however, with women accounting for 63 per cent of the total."
- **Target 4.a** (inclusive, disability-sensitive facilities): "Over a fifth of primary schools globally do not have access to basic services, such as electricity, drinking water and basic sanitation facilities."
- **Target 4.c** (qualified teachers): "In 2024, between 18 and 21% of teachers remain unqualified, depending on the level of education."

## Full target list (for reference)

4.1 primary/secondary completion · 4.2 early childhood education · 4.3 technical/vocational/tertiary access · 4.4 skills for employment · 4.5 eliminate gender/disparities (incl. disability) · 4.6 literacy/numeracy · 4.7 education for sustainable development · 4.a inclusive facilities · 4.b scholarships · 4.c qualified teachers.

## Summary

SDG 4 sets 2030 targets for education completion, access, and quality worldwide, and Target 4.5 is explicit that gaps must be closed for students facing barriers, including disability status. The official indicators are mostly global aggregates and do not break out figures for Los Angeles or South Korea specifically, so they should be used here as the global framework/benchmark our regional evidence responds to, not as directly comparable numbers.
