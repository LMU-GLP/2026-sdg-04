# LAUSD Board Resolution: "Recognizing and Addressing the Educational Implications of Dyslexia in LAUSD Schools"

- **Source:** Decoding Dyslexia CA, reporting on an LAUSD Board of Education resolution
- **URL:** https://decodingdyslexiaca.org/lausd-2nd-largest-school-district-in-the-country-passes-significant-dyslexia-resolution/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31
- **Note:** This page is secondary reporting on the resolution, not the primary resolution text itself (the primary PDF was not accessible). Treat the figures below as reported by Decoding Dyslexia CA.

## Key figures

- Resolution passed by the LAUSD Board of Education on **June 20, 2017**.
- "Up to **20% of the overall population** displays some signs or symptoms of dyslexia."
- Within LAUSD specifically, this is reported to translate to approximately **128,000 at-risk students**.

## What the resolution commits LAUSD to

- Reviewing and updating all district dyslexia-related policies within 90 days
- Clarifying that a "Specific Learning Disability" special-education eligibility determination does not prohibit also using the term "dyslexia"
- Developing teacher/staff training on dyslexia warning signs and evidence-based Structured Literacy instruction
- Providing accommodations and modifications for students with dyslexia
- Ensuring multisensory, direct, explicit, and structured reading instruction as part of Free and Appropriate Public Education (FAPE)

## Summary

In 2017, LAUSD's Board of Education passed a resolution acknowledging that up to 20% of the population shows signs of dyslexia — reported to mean roughly 128,000 at-risk students within the district — and committing the district to dyslexia-specific staff training, accommodations, and structured literacy instruction. This is the closest LA-specific dyslexia prevalence figure available to this wiki; it is a "signs or symptoms" estimate, not a clinically confirmed diagnosis count.
