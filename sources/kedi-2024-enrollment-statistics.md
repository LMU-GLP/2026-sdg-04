# 2024 교육기본통계 [2024 Basic Education Statistics]: Elementary & Middle School Enrollment

- **Source:** South Korea Ministry of Education / Korean Educational Development Institute (KEDI), 2024 교육기본통계 조사 결과, as reported by Korea Issue Journal
- **URL:** https://koreaissuejournal.co.kr/news/newsview.php?ncode=1065608435966078
- **Read on:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

- **"초등학교는 2,495,005명으로 108,924명(4.2%↓) 감소"** — Elementary school enrollment: **2,495,005 students**, down 108,924 (4.2%) from the prior year.
- **"중학교는 1,332,850명으로 6,019명(0.5%↑) 증가"** — Middle school enrollment: **1,332,850 students**, up 6,019 (0.5%) from the prior year — the only level of the two that grew.
- Combined elementary + middle school enrollment: **3,827,855 students** (2,495,005 + 1,332,850). This sum is our own arithmetic, not a figure stated directly in the source.

## Summary

South Korea's 2024 basic education statistics put nationwide elementary enrollment at just under 2.5 million (falling) and middle school enrollment at about 1.33 million (rising slightly) — a combined base of roughly 3.83 million students against which any national prevalence-rate estimate (e.g., for dyslexia or learning disabilities) can be scaled to a headcount.
