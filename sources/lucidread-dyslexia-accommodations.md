# LucidRead — Free Dyslexia Reading Accommodation Tool for California Schools

- **Source:** LucidRead (product/company website)
- **URL:** https://www.lucidread.org/california.html
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- "**Only 29% of California 4th graders reading at proficient level** (2024 NAEP)" — meaning roughly 71% are not reading proficiently by that measure.
- "**California was the 40th state to mandate universal dyslexia screening**."
- Relevant California laws cited: **AB 1369** (2015, statewide dyslexia guidelines), **SB 114** (2023, K–2 universal screening for reading difficulties including dyslexia risk), **SB 488** (2021, teacher preparation requirements), **AB 1454** (October 2025, credentialing standards for evidence-based reading instruction).

## What it does

LucidRead is a free Chrome browser extension providing dyslexia reading accommodations: text-to-speech, syllable and morpheme highlighting, "bionic reading," a picture dictionary, color overlays, customizable typography, a reading ruler, line tracking/focus, dark mode, and a vocabulary builder. It runs on Chromebooks districts already own, requires no account, and is positioned to fill the gap between California's screening mandate (which identifies at-risk readers) and the lack of any state-funded technology accommodation standard.

## Direct quotes

> "LucidRead eliminates the technology accommodation cost entirely: it is free, requires no accounts, and runs on the Chromebooks districts already own."

> "No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. LucidRead does not phone home."

## Summary

LucidRead is a free browser-based accommodation tool built specifically to answer a gap in California law: the state now requires screening students for dyslexia risk (SB 114) but funds no specific technology response, and its 2024 NAEP reading proficiency rate for 4th graders was just 29%. It's a concrete example of a product already trying to solve the "barriers at school" problem for reading-disabled students in the LA region's home state.
