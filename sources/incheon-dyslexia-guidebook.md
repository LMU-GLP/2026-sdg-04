# 난독증 진단과 지도 가이드북 (입문용) [Dyslexia Diagnosis and Instruction Guidebook (Introductory)]

- **Source:** 인천광역시교육청 기초학력지원센터 · 경인교육대학교 기초학력지원센터 (Incheon Metropolitan Office of Education Basic Academic Skills Support Center & Gyeongin National University of Education Basic Academic Skills Support Center). Publisher/lead author: 이대식 (Lee Dae-sik).

## Key figures

- **"전 세계적으로 전체 학생 중 적게는 5%에서 많게는 20% 정도의 학생이 난독증을 갖고 있는 것으로 추정되고 있습니다. 우리나라에서도 1~5% 정도의 학생이 난독증을 갖고 있을 것으로 추정되고 있습니다."** (Global estimate: 5–20% of all students have dyslexia. In Korea, an estimated 1–5% of students are believed to have dyslexia.)
- **Heritability:** "가족 중 부모 중 어느 한 쪽이라도 난독증이 있을 경우 그 자녀가 난독증을 가질 확률은 40~60% 정도입니다." (If either parent has dyslexia, the child's probability of having dyslexia is about 40–60%.) A sibling of a student with dyslexia has 3–10x higher odds of also having it.
- Incheon's own dyslexia-support ordinance (인천광역시 난독증 학생 지원 조례, 시행 2024.7.15.) defines dyslexia as normal intelligence, vision, and hearing, but a neurological difficulty processing language that impairs reading and comprehension.
- **Early identification matters mechanically, not just anecdotally:** a 2015 Connecticut longitudinal study (Shaywitz) cited in the guidebook shows the reading gap between strong and weak readers appears by grade 1 and *persists* through adolescence rather than closing on its own — the guidebook states dyslexia detected "초등학교 3학년이나 그 이후" (at 3rd grade or later) leaves a gap that requires substantially more time and effort to close.
- Recommended intensity of instruction: **"주당 최소 3회 이상"** (a minimum of 3 sessions per week) of systematic, explicit reading instruction, covering the five evidence-based components identified by the US National Reading Panel (2000): phonological awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary, reading comprehension.
- Screening tools available in Korean schools include a 1-minute oral reading fluency check, the web-based "한글 또박또박" test (5 minutes), and a national "읽기 학습 특성 체크리스트" (Reading Learning Characteristics Checklist) developed by the Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation for grades 1–6.

## Direct quotes

> "읽기의 어려움을 보이는 학생에 대한 정확하고 빠른 진단은 가장 강력하고도 효과적인 개입입니다." (Accurate, fast diagnosis of a student showing reading difficulty is the single most powerful and effective intervention.)

> "난독증은 개인에게 평생 영향을 미치지만 적절한 조기 개입 및 예방 프로그램을 통해 읽기 능력이 향상될 수 있습니다." (Dyslexia affects a person for life, but with appropriate early intervention and prevention programs, reading ability can improve.)

## Summary

This is a practical guidebook for Korean teachers and parents on identifying and instructing students with dyslexia, produced by a real regional support center rather than a national body — it estimates Korean dyslexia prevalence at 1–5% (versus a global 5–20% estimate), explicitly ties late identification (3rd grade or later) to a reading gap that doesn't close naturally, and prescribes concrete intervention frequency (3+ sessions/week) and screening tools already in use in Korean schools.
