# 2024 Basic Education Statistics (교육기본통계) — Elementary and Middle School Enrollment

- **Source:** Ministry of Education (교육부) & Korean Educational Development Institute (KEDI/한국교육개발원), "2024년 교육기본통계 조사 결과 발표" (2024 Basic Education Statistics survey results), released 2024-08-29. Figures confirmed via news coverage of the release: [THE 복지타임즈](https://www.bokjibang.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=40993) (2024-08-29); see also [KDI 경제교육·정보센터 summary](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=256623).
- **URL:** https://www.bokjibang.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=40993 (news report of the official MOE/KEDI release; the official release itself is at moe.go.kr/korea.kr as downloadable .hwp/.hwpx attachments not directly extractable as text)
- **Read on:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

- "초등학교는 2,495,005명으로 108,924명(4.2%↓) 감소" — **elementary school students: 2,495,005**, a decrease of 108,924 (4.2%) from the prior year.
- "중학교는 1,332,850명으로 6,019명(0.5%↑) 증가" — **middle school students: 1,332,850**, an increase of 6,019 (0.5%) from the prior year.
- Combined elementary + middle school enrollment: **3,827,855** (2,495,005 + 1,332,850 — arithmetic sum, not itself stated as a single figure in the source).
- Total enrollment across kindergarten through high school nationwide: **5,684,745**, down 98,867 (1.7%) year over year.

## Summary

South Korea's Ministry of Education/KEDI 2024 Basic Education Statistics release reports 2,495,005 elementary school students and 1,332,850 middle school students nationwide as of 2024, continuing a general decline in school-age population driven mostly by falling elementary enrollment. These are official national enrollment counts, not dyslexia or learning-disability figures — they are useful only as the population base for extrapolating from a prevalence rate reported elsewhere.
