# Khan Academy: Free, World-Class Education for Anyone, Anywhere

- **Source:** Khan Academy (official site)
- **URL:** https://www.khanacademy.org/about
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- Available in **190+ countries** and **55+ languages**.
- **227 million** total registered users.
- **75 billion** cumulative learning minutes on the platform.
- 2024–2025 school year: **104.9 million active learners**, **20.9 million new
  registrations**, **8.1 billion new learning minutes**.
- Khan Academy Districts (a program targeting under-resourced school districts)
  makes participating students **"6x more likely to reach recommended practice
  levels."**
- Khan Academy Kids is a free app for ages **2–8**, covering foundational
  preschool–grade 2 content.
- Khanmigo is an AI-powered tutor described as providing **"always-available"**
  support for students and teachers.
- Operates as a **501(c)(3) nonprofit**, and the platform is free across all core
  offerings.

## Direct quotes

> "Khan Academy's mission is to provide a free, world-class education to anyone, anywhere."

> "6x more likely to reach recommended practice levels" — Khan Academy Districts program

## What it targets specifically

The site frames its free-access model, translated content, and Districts/Khanmigo
programs as tools for reaching students who would otherwise face cost or
under-resourcing as barriers — not just a general study aid for students who
already have access.

## Summary

Khan Academy is a nonprofit offering a free platform used by over 227 million
registered users across 190+ countries and 55+ languages, with dedicated programs
(Districts, Kids, Khanmigo) aimed specifically at under-resourced students and
younger learners. It's an example of a program already operating at scale against
the cost and access barriers our challenge is about.
