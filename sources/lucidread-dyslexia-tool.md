# LucidRead: Free Reading-Accommodation Tool for California Schools

- **Source:** LucidRead product/policy page
- **URL:** https://www.lucidread.org/california.html
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **"2024 NAEP: Only 29% of California 4th graders reading at or above Proficient."**
- California was **"the 40th state to mandate universal dyslexia screening"** (K–2, beginning 2025–2026).
- California **allocated $40 million** for screening tools and teacher training as part of that mandate.

## Direct quotes

> "The law tells districts *what* to do. LucidRead provides a concrete, free, evidence-based answer to *how*."

> [LucidRead] "runs on the Chromebooks districts already own" and requires "no accounts" or data collection.

## What it is / what it does

LucidRead is a free Chrome extension built to accommodate three core dyslexic difficulties:
- **Decoding difficulty:** syllable splitting, morpheme highlighting, bionic reading, picture dictionary, text-to-speech, sentence simplification.
- **Visual stress:** color overlays, typography adjustments (including the Lexend font), dark mode, high contrast, line tracking.
- **Tracking loss:** reading ruler, line focus, bionic reading, progress bar.

It targets a specific gap: California mandates dyslexia *screening* in K–2, but sets no state-level mandate for which assistive-technology *accommodations* schools must then provide. LucidRead is aimed at K–12 students identified through screening or IEPs, general-education students who need reading support, and teachers implementing MTSS (Multi-Tiered System of Supports).

## Summary

LucidRead is a real, already-operating product that answers a specific, named gap in California law: the state requires dyslexia screening but not a specific accommodation response, and only 29% of California 4th graders read at or above NAEP "Proficient." It's a concrete example of a low-cost, technology-based intervention aimed at exactly the kind of reading-based barrier documented in South Korea's learning-disabilities research.
