# 251 Million Children and Youth Still Out of School, Despite Decades of Progress

- **Source:** UNESCO, Global Education Monitoring (GEM) Report 2024, reported via UN News
- **URL:** https://news.un.org/en/story/2024/10/1156366 (report announcement: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/251m-children-and-youth-still-out-school-despite-decades-progress-unesco-report)
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **251 million children and youth** remain out of school worldwide (GEM Report 2024).
- The out-of-school population has been reduced by **only 1%** in nearly 10 years since
  the SDGs were adopted in 2015.
- **110 million** more children and youth have entered school since 2015.
- **40 million** more young people have finished secondary school compared with 2015.
- **33%** of school-aged children and youth are out of school in low-income countries,
  versus **3%** in high-income countries.
- Investment gap: **$8,543 per learner** in high-income countries versus **$55 per
  learner** in low- and middle-income countries.
- Global aid to education declined from **9.3% (2019)** to **7.6% (2022)** of total aid.
- **Sub-Saharan Africa** hosts more than half of all out-of-school children and
  adolescents globally.

## Summary

UNESCO's 2024 Global Education Monitoring Report found 251 million children and
youth out of school worldwide, with only 1% progress in reducing that number since
2015. The gap is sharply unequal: a third of school-aged children in low-income
countries are out of school versus 3% in high-income countries, and rich countries
spend over 150 times more per learner.
