# SDG 4: Quality Education — Targets and Indicators

- **Source:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Sustainable Development
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- **Target 4.1** (free, equitable, quality primary/secondary education): global completion rates in 2024 reached **88% in primary, 78% in lower secondary, and 61% in upper secondary education.**
- **Target 4.2** (early childhood education): global participation in organized learning one year before primary entry **stagnated at around 75%.**
- **Target 4.5** (eliminate gender disparities, equal access for the vulnerable): wealth parity index in completion rates is **0.91 (primary), 0.68 (lower secondary), 0.34 (upper secondary)** — the gap between richest and poorest widens sharply at higher levels.
- **Target 4.6** (youth/adult literacy): **"754 million adults worldwide remained illiterate in 2024,"** with women accounting for 63% of that total.
- **Target 4.a** (safe, inclusive learning environments, disability-sensitive facilities): **"More than one in five primary schools globally still lack basic services"** such as electricity, safe drinking water, and sanitation.
- **Target 4.c** (qualified teachers): **"Around one in five teachers do not meet their country's minimum qualification standards"** globally.

## Direct quotes

> "By 2030, ensure that all girls and boys complete free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education leading to relevant and effective learning outcomes" (Target 4.1)

> "By 2030, build and upgrade education facilities that are child, disability and gender sensitive and provide safe, non-violent, inclusive and effective learning environments for all" (Target 4.a)

## Summary

SDG 4 sets 2030 targets for universal completion, literacy, and inclusive facilities, but the UN's own 2024 tracking shows persistent gaps: only 61% of the world's youth finish upper secondary, wealth is the sharpest predictor of who drops out, and one in five schools still lacks basic services like electricity or water. Target 4.a explicitly names disability-sensitive facilities as part of "quality education," which is directly relevant to any barrier tied to disability or learning difference.
