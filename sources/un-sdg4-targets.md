# SDG Goal 4: Quality Education — Targets and Indicators

- **Source:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Sustainable Development Goals
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures / targets (quoted from the site)

- **Target 4.1:** "Ensure that all girls and boys complete free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education leading to relevant and effective learning outcomes."
  - Indicator 4.1.1: Proportion of children and young people in grades 2/3, at end of primary, and at end of lower secondary achieving minimum proficiency in reading and mathematics, by sex.
  - Indicator 4.1.2: Completion rate (primary, lower secondary, upper secondary education).
- **Target 4.2:** "Ensure that all girls and boys have access to quality early childhood development, care and pre-primary education so they are ready for primary education."
- **Target 4.3:** "Ensure equal access for all women and men to affordable and quality technical, vocational and tertiary education, including university."
- **Target 4.4:** "Substantially increase the number of youth and adults who have relevant skills for employment, decent jobs and entrepreneurship."
  - Indicator 4.4.1: Proportion of youth and adults with information and communications technology (ICT) skills, by type of skill.
- **Target 4.5:** "Eliminate gender disparities in education and ensure equal access to all levels of education and vocational training for vulnerable groups."
- **Target 4.6:** "Ensure that all youth and a substantial proportion of adults achieve literacy and numeracy."
- **Target 4.7:** Ensure all learners acquire knowledge and skills to promote sustainable development, including "education for sustainable development and sustainable lifestyles, human rights, gender equality, promotion of a culture of peace and non-violence, global citizenship."
- **Target 4.a:** Build and upgrade education facilities that are child, disability and gender sensitive providing "safe, non-violent, inclusive and effective learning environments for all."
  - Indicator 4.a.1: Proportion of schools offering basic services, by type of service.
- **Target 4.b:** Substantially expand globally the number of scholarships available to developing countries for higher education enrollment.
- **Target 4.c:** Substantially increase the supply of qualified teachers through international cooperation for teacher training in developing countries.

## Note on scope

The site lists indicators (e.g., 4.1.1, 4.a.1) but does not itself publish country-level indicator values on this page. Any country-specific figures used elsewhere in this wiki come from other sources, not from this page.

## Summary

SDG 4 sets targets for equitable, quality education at every level, with Target 4.a explicitly calling out disability-sensitive, safe, and inclusive learning environments — the target most directly relevant to students who face barriers at school. Targets 4.1 and 4.6 (learning outcomes and literacy) are the ones our two candidate regions' evidence (chronic absenteeism in LA, reading/learning-disability identification in South Korea) most directly bears on.
