# Wiki index

Challenge: *How might we make learning more accessible, engaging, or useful for
students who face barriers at school?* (SDG 4)

Start here, then read the two region pages, then the comparison.

- **[SDG 4 targets](sdg4-targets.md)** — the ten UN targets under Goal 4, and which
  ones (4.5, 4.a, 4.1) map most directly onto "barriers at school."
- **[Global out-of-school children](global-out-of-school-children.md)** — 251
  million children and youth out of school worldwide (UNESCO 2024), and why
  that global crisis is a different kind of problem from either of our two
  candidate regions.
- **[LA: chronic absenteeism](la-chronic-absenteeism.md)** — LAUSD's chronic
  absenteeism rate fell from 45%+ (2021–22) to 32%+ (2023–24); what the district
  says is driving the improvement.
- **[South Korea: private education spending](korea-private-education-spending.md)**
  — Korean households spent $20.2B on private tutoring in 2024, a record 13.5%
  of monthly household budget by Q2 2025.
- **[LA vs. South Korea](la-vs-south-korea.md)** — the two regions' sources
  measure different things (attendance vs. spending); why they can't be ranked
  against each other, and what's missing to make them comparable.
- **[Khan Academy](khan-academy.md)** — a free, 190+-country nonprofit platform
  already working on cost/access barriers, as a reference point for solution
  design.
