# Wiki Index — SDG 4: Quality Education

Challenge: *How might we make learning more accessible, engaging, or useful for
students who face barriers at school?* Region not yet chosen — start with
[Los Angeles vs. South Korea](la-vs-south-korea.md), then read whichever region-specific
pages matter most to your argument.

| Page | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [SDG 4 Targets](sdg4-targets.md) | The official UN targets this challenge maps to, especially Target 4.5 (barriers/disability access) — global benchmarks, not regional data. |
| [LA: Chronic Absenteeism](la-chronic-absenteeism.md) | LAUSD's chronic-absenteeism rate (45%→32%, 2021–2024) and the low-tech home-visit program behind the improvement. |
| [Korea: Private Education Pressure](korea-private-education-pressure.md) | Record 13.5% of household spending now goes to private tutoring — a cost/time barrier distinct from learning disabilities. |
| [Dyslexia and Learning Disabilities](dyslexia-learning-disabilities.md) | South Korea's LD identification rate collapsed from 8.7% to 1.5% (2009–2019) even as 1–5% of students are estimated to struggle with reading — the clearest documented "invisible barrier" in this wiki. |
| [Dyslexia Support Tools](dyslexia-support-tools.md) | LucidRead, a free California browser extension, and how it compares to Korea's government guidebook as two live answers to the same underlying problem. |
| [Los Angeles vs. South Korea](la-vs-south-korea.md) | Side-by-side of both regions' evidence — and an explicit note on which figures can't be compared to each other. |
