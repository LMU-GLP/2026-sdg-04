# Khan Academy: a program already working on this

Khan Academy's stated mission is "to provide a free, world-class education to
anyone, anywhere" [[Khan Academy]](../sources/khan-academy.md).

## Scale

- Available in **190+ countries** and **55+ languages**
- **227 million** total registered users, **75 billion** cumulative learning
  minutes
- 2024–2025 school year: **104.9 million** active learners, **20.9 million** new
  registrations, **8.1 billion** new learning minutes

[[Khan Academy]](../sources/khan-academy.md)

## Programs aimed specifically at barriers, not just general study help

- **Khan Academy Districts**, aimed at under-resourced school districts, makes
  participating students **"6x more likely to reach recommended practice
  levels"** [[Khan Academy]](../sources/khan-academy.md).
- **Khan Academy Kids** is a free app for ages 2–8 covering preschool–grade 2
  content [[Khan Academy]](../sources/khan-academy.md).
- **Khanmigo**, an AI tutor, is described as providing **"always-available"**
  support for students and teachers [[Khan Academy]](../sources/khan-academy.md).
- It operates as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, free across all core offerings
  [[Khan Academy]](../sources/khan-academy.md).

## Relevance to our challenge

Khan Academy is evidence that a free, translated, at-scale response to cost and
under-resourcing barriers is possible and already operating — useful as a
reference point when we design our own concept, whether or not we end up
proposing something Khan-Academy-shaped. It doesn't address LA's
attendance/chronic-absenteeism barrier or South Korea's admissions-pressure
dynamic directly; those need their own program design.
See [LA vs. South Korea](la-vs-south-korea.md) and
[SDG 4 targets](sdg4-targets.md).
