# SDG 4 targets, and which ones this challenge is about

Our challenge — *"How might we make learning more accessible, engaging, or useful
for students who face barriers at school?"* — sits inside the UN's Sustainable
Development Goal 4, "quality education." The goal has ten targets
[[UN SDG 4]](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md).

## The targets that name barriers directly

- **Target 4.5** commits to eliminate "gender disparities in education and ensure
  equal access to all levels of education and vocational training for the
  vulnerable, including persons with disabilities, indigenous peoples and children
  in vulnerable situations" [[UN SDG 4]](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md). This is
  the target closest to "students who face barriers."
- **Target 4.a** commits to "build and upgrade education facilities that are
  child, disability and gender sensitive and provide safe, non-violent, inclusive
  and effective learning environments for all"
  [[UN SDG 4]](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md).

## The targets closer to "engaging or useful"

- **Target 4.1**: primary and secondary education should lead to "relevant and
  effective learning outcomes," not just seat time
  [[UN SDG 4]](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md).
- **Target 4.4**: substantially increasing "relevant skills, including technical
  and vocational skills, for employment" [[UN SDG 4]](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md).

## Every target, for reference

4.1 (completion), 4.2 (early childhood education), 4.3 (technical/tertiary access),
4.4 (skills for employment), 4.5 (gender/disability/vulnerability equity),
4.6 (literacy and numeracy), 4.7 (education for sustainable development),
4.a (safe facilities), 4.b (scholarships, target year 2020), 4.c (teacher supply).
Full verbatim text of each is in the source file
[[UN SDG 4]](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md).
