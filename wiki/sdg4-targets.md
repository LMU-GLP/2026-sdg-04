# UN SDG 4 Targets Relevant to Our Challenge

The full list of SDG 4 targets and indicators is in the [source file](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md). The ones most relevant to "students who face barriers at school":

- **Target 4.1** — all children completing free, quality primary/secondary education with "relevant and effective learning outcomes," measured partly by minimum reading/math proficiency (Indicator 4.1.1). This is the target our reading-proficiency and reading-disability evidence speaks to most directly ([LA/CA reading data](../sources/lucidread-dyslexia-accommodations.md); [Korea LD data](../sources/korea-learning-disabilities-prevalence.md)).
- **Target 4.5** — eliminating disparities in education access "for vulnerable groups," which would include both economically disadvantaged families (relevant to [Korea's private-education cost burden](../sources/korea-private-education.md)) and students with disabilities.
- **Target 4.a** — school environments that are "child, disability and gender sensitive," providing "safe, non-violent, inclusive and effective learning environments for all" — the target most directly about removing school-based barriers for disabled and at-risk students, relevant to both the [Korea dyslexia identification gap](../sources/korea-learning-disabilities-prevalence.md) and the accommodations described in the [Incheon guidebook](../sources/incheon-dyslexia-guidebook.md).

No source in this wiki gives an official UN indicator *value* (e.g., an actual 4.1.1 measurement) for either Los Angeles or South Korea — the UN page itself only defines the targets and indicators, not country data ([source](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md)).
