# SDG 4 Targets: What "Quality Education" Is Measured Against

Our challenge — accessibility, engagement, and usefulness of learning for students facing
barriers — sits inside the UN's Sustainable Development Goal 4. The UN tracks progress
against named targets, not vague aspirations, so these are the yardsticks a project in this
space should be measured against.

## The targets that matter most for "barriers at school"

- **Target 4.1** (complete, quality primary/secondary education): global completion rates
  in 2024 were **88% primary, 78% lower secondary, 61% upper secondary** — meaning nearly
  4 in 10 students who start school don't finish upper secondary.
  ([UN SDG4 targets](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md))
- **Target 4.5** (eliminate disparities for the vulnerable): the wealth parity index falls
  from **0.91 at primary level to 0.34 at upper secondary** — the poorest students are
  roughly a third as likely as the richest to finish upper secondary.
  ([UN SDG4 targets](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md))
- **Target 4.a** (child- and disability-sensitive facilities): **"more than one in five
  primary schools globally still lack basic services"** like electricity, water, or
  sanitation. ([UN SDG4 targets](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md))
- **Target 4.c** (qualified teachers): **"around one in five teachers do not meet their
  country's minimum qualification standards."** ([UN SDG4 targets](../sources/un-sdg4-targets.md))

## Why this matters for our angle

Target 4.a is the one that most directly names disability as a design requirement for
"quality education," not an afterthought. That gives explicit UN backing to a project framed
around students with reading disabilities or learning differences — see
[Dyslexia and Learning Disabilities](dyslexia-learning-disabilities.md) and
[Reading-Support Technology](reading-support-technology.md) for evidence that this exact
gap exists in both of our candidate regions.

**Note on scope:** the UN figures here are global averages. Nothing on this page should be
read as a claim about Los Angeles or South Korea specifically — see
[LA vs. South Korea](la-vs-korea-comparison.md) for those regions' own numbers.
